# Trademark & Branding Policy

Harvester is independent software. It is **not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored
by** any third-party platform, server software, or service it integrates with.

## Naming and branding rules

- Harvester's own name, logo, and branding must never incorporate the name or logo of any
  third-party platform it connects to.
- Any third-party service name used in documentation, setup screens, or configuration
  fields (e.g. a field asking for a server token) is purely descriptive/functional — it
  identifies what the field or feature does, not an endorsement or partnership.
- Harvester's own branding must never be displayed as part of, or styled to resemble, a
  third-party platform's own look and feel.
- Do not use any third-party logo anywhere in Harvester's UI, marketing, or store listing
  without that party's written permission.

## Required disclaimer

Public-facing materials (README, store listing, "About" screen) should carry a short,
generic disclaimer along these lines:

> Harvester is independent software. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored
> by the platforms or services it connects to. All third-party trademarks referenced belong
> to their respective owners.

This policy is written to stay valid regardless of which platforms Harvester integrates
with today or adds support for later.
